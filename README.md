### what it does

- **role esp** — murderer red, sheriff blue, hero gold, everyone else grey, with names and distance
- **dropped gun** — a marker on the gun when the sheriff dies, grab it with **g**
- **auto grab** — its own switch (**h**): picks the gun up for you, but waits if the murderer is standing near it
- **coin farm** — tweens under the map and pokes up under each coin, plans the shortest route through the coins, two presets (peek and direct), stops when your bag is full and can reset you
- **aim lock** — hold **q**: sticks to the murderer (or the sheriff when you're the murderer), works with right-click and first person, their esp turns white
- **fling** — **z** murderer, **x** sheriff, **c** anyone you pick
- **teleports** — map spawns, lobby, the dropped gun, the nearest coin, the murderer, the sheriff, any player, a saved spot and back
- **anti afk** — taps o/i after a few quiet minutes so roblox doesn't kick you
- **live hud** — who the murderer and sheriff are, your role and your bag at a glance
- **discord + dashboard** — candy/coins this round and session, per hour, best round, time to fill, who's murderer and sheriff, a post every round, a ping if you get disconnected

### how to use

**p** opens the menu · **f1** esp · **f2** coin farm · **g** grab the gun · **h** auto grab · hold **q** to aim · **z**/**x**/**c** fling.
every keybind can be changed in the menu. settings save on their own.

### discord

connect it at [adorablewhale.world](https://adorablewhale.world) > dashboard > discord, pick **MM2** and paste a webhook (or pick a bot channel). the script never sees the url. what it sends: your roblox name and avatar, coins, rounds, full bags, deaths, map and session time. nothing is sent until you set it up.
